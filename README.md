# Elly Okinyo

Economist, researcher and data analyst based in Helsinki. I work on development, macroeconomics and labour markets, with a focus on time series, forecasting and reproducible analysis in R, Stata and Python. I care about getting the method right and then explaining it plainly.

I write about economics and economic policy, from Kenya to Finland, at [ellyokinyo.com](https://ellyokinyo.com). Each data article comes with public code that reproduces its numbers and charts.

## Selected work

- **[A record month, a shrinking US corridor: what Kenya's 2026 remittance data are telling us](https://ellyokinyo.com/writing/kenya-remittances-2026.html)** (September 2026)
  Central Bank of Kenya remittance data: a record month in August, a flat year overall and a shrinking US corridor.
  Code: [kenya-remittances-2026](https://github.com/eokinyo/kenya-remittances-2026)

- **[The 1% US tax is not the biggest cost of sending money to Kenya](https://ellyokinyo.com/writing/kenya-remittance-costs-2026.html)** (October 2026)
  World Bank remittance price data: what it costs to send money to Kenya, by corridor and payment method.
  Code: [kenya-remittance-costs](https://github.com/eokinyo/kenya-remittance-costs)

- **Does Google Trends Improve Bitcoin Volatility Forecasts?** (MSc thesis, University of Helsinki, 2026)
  GARCH-family models compared with Random Forest regression for one-step-ahead Bitcoin volatility forecasts, with Google Trends search interest as a measure of investor attention.
  Code: [Thesis_Work](https://github.com/eokinyo/Thesis_Work) · Thesis: [Helda](https://helda.helsinki.fi/items/54af4cb8-7493-4951-850e-5ba7c10e6deb)
